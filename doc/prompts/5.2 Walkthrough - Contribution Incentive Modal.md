# 5.2 Walkthrough - First-Visit Contribution Incentive Modal for QBank & Donate

Implemented an introductory modal shown automatically the first time a visitor navigates to either the **Question Bank (`#Qbank`)** or **Donate Resources (`#Donate`)** section. The modal communicates the reciprocal "donation" cycle of past exam questions and study notes to foster community-driven student contributions.

---

## 1. Key Highlights & Changes

### A. Introductory Contribution Modal UI (`index.html` & `style.css`)
- **Markup Location**: Placed `#contribution-modal` in [`index.html`](file:///d:/GitHub/[oU1TS]/b1t-Acad/index.html) adjacent to sitewide dialogs.
- **Glassmorphic Presentation**: Designed with 16px rounded corners, deep drop shadows, subtle border glow (`rgba(58, 162, 231, 0.3)`), and frosted-glass backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`).
- **Verbatim Motivational Messaging**:
  - **English Callout Card**:
    > *"Attention!! If you were ever benefited from this Project, please consider contributing by donating Questions/Notes or spreading the word! Appreciated 👍"*
  - **Bengali Narrative**: Thoughtful Bengali prose explaining that exam questions visible today exist only because seniors previously took a moment to share them, and that keeping this ecosystem alive for future juniors is everyone's shared responsibility.
  - **Islamic Benediction**:
    > *"JazakAllahu Khairan. BarakAllahu Feekum."*
- **Action Elements**:
  - Protruding circular close button (`#close-contribution-modal`) with hover rotation and scaling.
  - Primary *"Understood / বুঝেছি 👍"* dismiss button.
  - Quick-action link *"Donate Questions / Notes"* redirecting visitors to `#Donate`.

### B. Intelligent Visit Detection & Re-open Access (`js/contribution-modal.js`)
- **First-Visit Auto Popup**: Tracks visitor state using `localStorage.getItem('b1t_contribution_intro_seen')`. Triggers upon the visitor's first arrival at `#Qbank` or `#Donate` (whether via direct URL hash, dynamic hash changes, or navigation link clicks).
- **Single Sitewide Display**: Once displayed or acknowledged, it will not interrupt the visitor again.
- **Manual Re-open Triggers (`.btn-why-donate`)**: Added dedicated pill buttons inside both `#Qbank` and `#Donate`:
  - Question Bank: *"💡 Why contribute? / কেন অবদান রাখবেন?"*
  - Donate Resources: *"❤️ Why contribute? / কেন অবদান রাখবেন?"*
  Visitors can click these at any time to re-read the message on demand.
- **Backdrop & Keyboard Dismissal**: Clicking outside the modal container or pressing `Escape` closes the dialog smoothly.

### C. Theme & Responsive Design (`style.css`)
- **Dark Theme (Default)**: Deep obsidian cards with sky-blue accents and gold attention highlights.
- **Gray / Light Theme (`[data-theme="gray"]`)**: Clean high-contrast white card container, dark slate typography (`#1e293b`), and sapphire blue accents (`#1976d2`).
- **Mobile Viewports ($\le 768\text{px}$ & $\le 480\text{px}$)**: Fluid modal sizing (`calc(100% - 16px)`), touch-friendly buttons, scaled padding, and smooth scrolling for smaller screens.
